Warehouse Attendance Logger

Paste WhatsApp attendance → Preview → Phase (add to batch) → Download Excel report

1. Paste one or more WhatsApp messages

Absent = the word "absent"/"abs", an ABSENT heading, or a ❌ mark. Casual = a CASUALS heading, the word "casual", or a line like "Casuals 5". You can paste several messages at once (each starting with its title/date line). Messages with the same date and shift are merged, so attendance from different messages is never lost. Date and shift can be edited per message in the preview. Tap a Status or Type to correct it.

3. Phased batch

The report has separate sheets: Attendance (absent rows in red), Summary (totals of present, absent, casuals), Absent, Casuals and By Person.
